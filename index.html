Cash matching

About

I started in investment banking at J.P. Morgan after a JD/MBA at Emory. Since then I have run finance at venture-backed and PE-backed software and fintech companies.

At Hearth I went from finance associate to VP of Finance. We took the company from $1.5M of monthly burn to positive cash flow, and I built the model for the $23M Series B. At TradeCentric I ran strategic finance for a $20M ARR business and cut commission processing from five days to same day.

At Haystack I am the whole finance function - QuickBooks, Stripe, HubSpot, and Mercury feed one BigQuery warehouse across three entities. I book 96% of entries by day three. I work in English and Spanish.
