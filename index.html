Automations

Cash matchingCommissionsConsolidationCloseRevenue analyticsCall signals

Cash matching

Bank receipts are matched against open invoices and scored by amount, payer, and timing. Confident matches queue for one-click approval; exceptions go to review. Nothing posts without a person signing off.

Commissions

Payouts calculated straight from CRM and billing data, with twice-monthly approval routing to managers. Built once before at TradeCentric across five comp plans.

Five days of processing → same day

Multi-entity consolidation

QuickBooks, Stripe, HubSpot, and Mercury land in BigQuery and roll up across three legal entities into reconciled reporting packs, with no spreadsheet stitching.

Close automation

Recurring journal entries, accruals, and reconciliations prepared from the warehouse so the close runs in-house on a fixed calendar.

Median JE posting 11–15 days → 2; 96% booked by day 3

Revenue analytics

ARR, retention, and cohort views rebuilt from billing and CRM data, so board numbers tie back to source transactions.

Call signals

Sales call transcripts scored for sentiment and buying signals in dbt, then written back to the CRM so pipeline reviews start from evidence.

About

I started in investment banking at J.P. Morgan after a JD/MBA at Emory, then spent the next several years running finance at venture- and PE-backed software and fintech companies.

At Hearth I went from finance associate to VP of Finance, helped turn $1.5M of monthly burn into positive cash flow, and led the modeling for a $23M Series B. At TradeCentric I ran strategic finance for a $20M ARR business and cut commission processing from five days to same-day.

Today I'm VP of Finance at Haystack, the only person in finance, accounting, and operations. I build the tools that make that work: QuickBooks, Stripe, HubSpot, and Mercury consolidated in BigQuery across three entities, with a close that books 96% of entries by day three.
