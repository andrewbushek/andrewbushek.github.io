About

I started in investment banking at J.P. Morgan after a JD/MBA at Emory, then spent the next several years running finance at venture- and PE-backed software and fintech companies.

At Hearth I went from finance associate to VP of Finance, helped turn $1.5M of monthly burn into positive cash flow, and led the modeling for a $23M Series B. At TradeCentric I ran strategic finance for a $20M ARR business and cut commission processing from five days to same-day.

Today I'm VP of Finance at Haystack, the only person in finance, accounting, and operations. I build the tools that make that work: QuickBooks, Stripe, HubSpot, and Mercury consolidated in BigQuery across three entities, with a close that books 96% of entries by day three.
